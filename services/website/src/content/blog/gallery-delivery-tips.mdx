# 5 Tips for Client Gallery Delivery That Impress

The moment you deliver a gallery to your client is crucial. It's not just about sharing photos—it's about creating an experience that reinforces why they chose you and encourages them to recommend you to others.

Here are five proven techniques to make your gallery deliveries unforgettable.

## 1. Set the Stage with a Preview Teaser

Don't just dump 500 photos into a gallery and send a link. Build anticipation:

**Before the full gallery:**
- Send 5-10 sneak peek images via email or social media
- Time this for maximum impact (2-3 days after the event)
- Use phrases like "Wait until you see the rest..."

**Pro Tip:** Use vDrive's scheduled sharing to automatically release teasers before the full gallery goes live.

## 2. Curate Before You Deliver

Your clients hired you for your eye, not just your camera. Exercise editorial judgment:

### What to Include
- Every key moment captured
- Multiple angles of important scenes
- Group and individual portraits
- Detail shots that tell the story

### What to Exclude
- Near-duplicates (keep only the best of each series)
- Technically flawed shots (unless irreplaceable)
- Unflattering angles (use your judgment)

**Aim for:** 50-100 photos per hour of shooting, depending on the event type.

## 3. Organize for Easy Navigation

A massive gallery with no organization overwhelms clients. Help them enjoy the experience:

### Smart Organization Techniques

1. **Chronological Albums**
   - Getting Ready
   - Ceremony
   - Cocktail Hour
   - Reception
   - Send-off

2. **AI-Powered Face Grouping**
   Let vDrive automatically group photos by the people in them. Perfect for large events where guests want to find themselves.

3. **Highlight Album**
   Create a "Best Of" album with 20-30 standout images at the top.

## 4. Personalize the Experience

Generic delivery emails feel transactional. Make it personal:

### Customize Your Message

❌ "Your gallery is ready. Click here to view."

✅ "Sarah & Michael, I've been so excited to share these with you! Your ceremony brought tears to my eyes—especially the moment during the vows. I hope these photos bring back every beautiful feeling from your day."

### Brand Your Gallery

- Use your logo and brand colors
- Set a custom gallery URL
- Add a personal welcome message inside the gallery

## 5. Make Downloads Effortless

Nothing kills the experience faster than technical friction:

### Optimize for Everyone

- **Mobile-friendly**: Most clients view on phones first
- **One-click downloads**: Enable individual and bulk downloads
- **Multiple resolutions**: Offer web-sized for social and full-res for printing
- **Clear instructions**: Include a "How to Download" note

### Pro Tip: Smart Delivery Options

Configure vDrive to offer:
- **High-res downloads** for print-quality images
- **Social-optimized versions** sized for Instagram/Facebook
- **Print-ready cropped versions** for standard frame sizes

## Bonus: Follow Up for Reviews

The delivery moment is when emotions are highest. Capitalize on it:

1. **Include a review request** in your delivery message
2. **Make it easy** with direct links to Google/Yelp/The Knot
3. **Time it right** - send a gentle reminder 1 week after viewing
