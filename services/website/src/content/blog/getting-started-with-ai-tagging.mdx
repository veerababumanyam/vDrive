# Getting Started with AI Photo Tagging

One of the most powerful features of RawDrive is our AI-powered photo tagging system. In this guide, we'll walk you through how it works and how to get the most out of it.

## How AI Tagging Works

When you upload photos to RawDrive, our AI analyzes each image to identify:

- **Scenes**: Wedding, portrait, landscape, indoor, outdoor
- **Objects**: People, animals, vehicles, buildings
- **Colors**: Dominant color palette
- **Emotions**: Happy, serious, candid moments

This happens automatically in the background, so by the time you're ready to organize your photos, they're already tagged.

## Finding Photos Quickly

Once your photos are tagged, finding specific images is effortless:

1. Use the search bar to type what you're looking for
2. Filter by tags in the sidebar
3. Combine multiple filters for precise results

For example, searching "outdoor portrait smiling" will show you all outdoor portraits where the subject is smiling.

## Tips for Better Results

- **Upload high-quality images**: Better image quality leads to more accurate tagging
- **Be patient with large uploads**: AI processing runs in the background
- **Review and refine**: You can always add or remove tags manually

## What's Next?

Now that your photos are organized, you can:

- Create smart albums that auto-update based on tags
- Share galleries with clients using magic links
- Use face recognition to let clients find themselves
