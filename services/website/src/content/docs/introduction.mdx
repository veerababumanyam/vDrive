# Introduction to RawDrive

Welcome to RawDrive, the AI-powered photography platform designed for professional photographers. This guide will help you understand the core concepts and get started quickly.

## What is RawDrive?

RawDrive is an all-in-one platform that helps professional photographers:

- **Manage** thousands of photos with AI-powered organization
- **Create** beautiful, branded client galleries
- **Deliver** photos with confidence using magic links
- **Collaborate** with team members and clients

## Core Concepts

### Workspaces

A workspace is your photography business hub. Each workspace contains:

- Your photo library
- Client galleries
- Team members
- Settings and branding

You can have multiple workspaces for different businesses or brands.

### Galleries

Galleries are collections of photos you share with clients. Each gallery has:

- Customizable theme and branding
- Privacy settings (public, password, private)
- Download permissions
- Client proofing features

### AI Features

RawDrive uses artificial intelligence to:

- **Auto-tag** photos with scenes, objects, and colors
- **Recognize faces** across thousands of images
- **Suggest** the best photos from a set
- **Organize** your library automatically
