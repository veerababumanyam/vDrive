# Getting Started with vDrive

Welcome to vDrive! This guide will walk you through setting up your account and getting started with managing your photography galleries.

## Creating Your Account

1. **Sign Up**: Visit [app.vdrive.io/sign-up](https://app.vdrive.io/sign-up) and enter your email address
2. **Verify Email**: Check your inbox and click the verification link
3. **Set Up Your Profile**: Add your name and studio information
4. **Choose a Plan**: Start with our free tier or select a plan that fits your needs

## Setting Up Your Workspace

Your workspace is where all your galleries, photos, and client information live.

### Workspace Settings

Configure your workspace with:

- **Studio Name**: Your business name as it appears to clients
- **Custom Domain**: Use your own domain for branded galleries
- **Brand Colors**: Set primary and accent colors for your galleries
- **Logo**: Upload your studio logo

### Team Members

Invite team members with different roles:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| Owner | Full access to all features and billing |
| Admin | Manage galleries, clients, and team members |
| Editor | Upload and edit photos, manage galleries |
| Viewer | View galleries and download approved photos |

## Creating Your First Gallery

Once your workspace is set up, you're ready to create your first gallery:

```
1. Click "New Gallery" in your dashboard
2. Name your gallery and set privacy options
3. Upload your photos (drag & drop or browse)
4. Wait for AI processing to complete
5. Share the magic link with your client
```

### Photo Upload Tips

- **Supported formats**: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, RAW (CR2, NEF, ARW)
- **Maximum file size**: 100MB per photo
- **Batch upload**: Up to 500 photos at once
- **Auto-optimize**: Photos are automatically optimized for web viewing
